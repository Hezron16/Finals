Emergency Kit Guide

Essential Go-Bag Supplies for Every Disaster.

Water & Food

About 4 liters of water per person per day and a 3-day supply of ready-to-eat, non-perishable food.

First Aid

Bandages, antiseptic, personal medicines, thermometer, gloves and masks.

Radio & Power

Battery or hand-crank radio, flashlight, spare batteries, power bank and a whistle for each family member.

Important Documents

IDs, insurance papers, titles and medical records in a waterproof bag, plus some cash.

Hygiene & Sanitation

Soap, alcohol or sanitizer, wipes, toothbrush, menstrual pads and garbage bags.

Clothing & Shelter

Sturdy shoes, extra clothes, raincoat or poncho and a blanket.

Pack for 3 daysPlan for at least three days without help or electricity.
Keep it within reachStore the bag near an exit where anyone can grab it fast.
Check twice a yearSwap out expired food, water and medicines.

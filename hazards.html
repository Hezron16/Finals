Disaster Guides

Typhoon Guide

Typhoons bring strong winds, heavy rain, storm surge and landslides.

Before

  • Know your area's risks and the nearest evacuation center.
  • Secure the roof, windows and loose objects outside.
  • Charge phones and power banks and pack your Go Bag.
  • Follow PAGASA bulletins and your local government unit (LGU).

During

  • Stay indoors, away from windows and glass doors.
  • Evacuate right away if your LGU orders it or you live in a flood, landslide or storm-surge area.
  • Switch off the main power if water enters the house.
  • Keep a battery radio on for updates.

After

  • Wait for the official all-clear before going outside.
  • Stay away from floodwater, fallen trees and downed power lines.
  • Check your home for damage before going back in.
  • Drink only safe water until your supply is cleared.

Flood Guide

Floods can rise within minutes and carry diseases and debris.

Before

  • Learn which streets in your area flood and where higher ground is.
  • Keep documents and valuables in waterproof bags on high shelves.
  • Prepare your Go Bag and agree on a family meeting point.
  • Move early when water starts to rise.

During

  • Go to higher ground. Do not wait for the water to reach your door.
  • Do not walk, swim or drive through moving floodwater.
  • Turn off electricity at the main switch if it is safe to do so.
  • Avoid contact with floodwater to lower the risk of leptospirosis.

After

  • Return home only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Wear boots and gloves when cleaning up.
  • See a doctor if you waded in floodwater and feel feverish or achy.
  • Throw away food that touched floodwater.

Earthquake Guide

Earthquakes strike without warning, so practice before they happen.

Before

  • Fasten heavy furniture and shelves to walls.
  • Identify a sturdy table or desk and clear exits in every room.
  • Practice Duck, Cover and Hold with your family.
  • Keep shoes, a flashlight and your Go Bag near your bed.

During

  • DROP to your hands and knees, take COVER under a sturdy table and HOLD ON.
  • Stay away from windows, glass and heavy objects that can fall.
  • If outside, move to an open area away from buildings, poles and wires.
  • Never use elevators. Near the coast, if shaking is strong or long, move to higher ground right away.

After

  • Expect aftershocks and be ready to drop, cover and hold again.
  • Check yourself and others for injuries, and check for gas leaks and damage.
  • Leave damaged buildings. Use text messages instead of calls.
  • Follow advisories from PHIVOLCS and your LGU.

Fire Guide

Most home fires can be prevented, and a clear escape plan saves lives.

Before

  • Install smoke alarms and keep a fire extinguisher where you can reach it.
  • Plan two ways out of every room and practice them.
  • Do not overload outlets. Turn off LPG tanks and stoves when not in use.
  • Keep emergency numbers where everyone can see them.

During

  • Shout to warn everyone, get out, then call 911 or your local fire station.
  • Crawl low under smoke. Feel doors with the back of your hand before opening.
  • If clothes catch fire: stop, drop and roll.
  • Use stairs, never elevators. Once you are out, stay out.

After

  • Do not go back inside until firefighters say it is safe.
  • Get medical care for burns and smoke inhalation.
  • Contact your LGU or barangay for help with shelter and supplies.
  • Do not use electricity or gas until they have been checked.

Safety Reminders

  • Keep an emergency kit ready.
  • Know your evacuation routes.
  • Stay updated with official news (PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, NDRRMC, your LGU).
  • Save emergency numbers on paper, not only on your phone.
  • Charge phones and power banks before a storm.
  • Check on neighbors who are elderly, sick or living alone.

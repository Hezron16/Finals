Frequently Asked Questions

How to use this portal?

Pick a hazard on Choose Hazard to read what to do before, during and after it. Then tick off supplies on My Checklist, see what to pack in the Kit Guide, and write your Family Plan.

What to do in an emergency?

Stay calm and get to a safe place first. Follow instructions from your LGU, then call 911 if someone is in danger. Keep a radio on for official updates.

Contacting authorities

See the emergency hotlines on this page. Your barangay hall and local DRRM office can tell you about evacuation centers near you.

What is a resilient community?

A community where people know the risks, have a plan, help each other and can recover quickly after a disaster.

Is HandaPH free to use?

Yes. HandaPH is a student project that shares free preparedness information for Filipino families.

Where does the information come from?

Guides are based on advice from PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, NDRRMC and UNICEF Philippines. See the References page. Always follow official local advisories first.
